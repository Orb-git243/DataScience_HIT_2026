MyAnimeList Anime Data:

This dataset contains comprehensive information on anime gathered from MyAnimeList (MAL), one of the world's largest active anime and manga communities. It includes a wide range of data points, including broadcast details, genres, production studios, user scores, and engagement metrics.

The dataset consists of a single CSV file (animes.csv).
